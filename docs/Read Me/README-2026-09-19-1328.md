## 6. #95 — a real, active data-loss defect found and filed as Blocking

Investigating why 32 new photos had "no guess" led to a much bigger
finding: **eBird CSV re-import silently nulls previously-confirmed
`Match.observation_id`, without ever re-linking to the newly-recreated
`Observation` row for the same species.** Root cause confirmed in
`ebird_import.py:108-121`. Since eBird's standard export is cumulative
(entire history, not just new checklists), any full re-upload --
completely normal usage -- silently reverts previously-confirmed matches
back to "Unmatched," with no error or warning.

Jen pushed back on the first pass at scoping this (Smith Island, July
12) -- correctly. A deeper look found that outing is actually two
checklists (40 and 41) sharing one outing group; checklist 41 held the
real Tufted Puffin data all along, and the app's own multi-checklist
grouping was already correctly surfacing it. That specific case was a
false alarm, not damage. Redid the full-database scope check afterward
with a stricter test (does a surviving match exist anywhere for the same
photo) -- corrected count: **50 matches genuinely broken across 8
checklists** (not the initially-reported 51/9), spanning 2026-08-12
through today, including one live proof case from this exact session
(#79's own test photo, whose `note` survived while its species link
didn't -- confirming the exact mechanism in real time). Filed as **#95**,
status Blocking. Jen decided the actual fix is too much for today's
session; deferred, but tracked as blocking further test-data-driven work.
