## 2. Locked in the matching strategy — and why

Resolved the parked checklist-first vs. Claude-first debate as **hybrid**:
checklist-first stays the default, Claude-first only runs as a fallback
when there's no checklist match, or when a checklist matched but the
constrained guess came back empty. The deciding factor wasn't just
cost/accuracy — you wanted Claude's use in the app to read as a
deliberate, narrow engineering decision, not something forced in to check
an "I used AI" box.

## 4. Matching logic: found and fixed a real bug, mapped the rest

- **Discovered:** four separate `7/12` Smith Island checklists all
  surfaced the same Tufted Puffin photo as a "matched" candidate, since
  checklist-to-photo matching runs independently per checklist.
- **Fixed (designed):** group checklists by **date + eBird Location ID**
  before matching, not a GPS radius — a fixed radius can't work for both
  a half-mile park loop and several miles of open water, but eBird's own
  Location ID already encodes "one place" at whatever scale it actually
  is.
- **Resolved:** approving an eBird-gap suggestion never writes back to
  eBird (its API doesn't support that anyway) — it labels the photo only,
  with a manual link to the real checklist on eBird.org.
- **New concept:** checklist-less photo clusters (grouped by date + GPS
  proximity, since there's no Location ID to lean on without a checklist
  at all) — every photo now has *somewhere* to live in the UI, even with
  zero matching checklists.
- **New concept:** the three-bucket structure for a single trip — photos
  with a match, photos without a match, and (new) birds eBird says you
  saw with no photo to back it up. Captured as structure only; UX not
  designed yet.
- **New, unresolved:** batches of same-species photos (many Tufted
  Puffins from one trip) raise both an efficiency problem (many manual
  per-photo clicks) and an accuracy problem (each photo judged in
  isolation). Suggested but not locked in: send a *burst* of related
  photos to Claude in one call instead of one at a time.
- **Found during wrap-up reflection:** two more open corner cases —
  time zone handling in the time-window matcher (not hypothetical; your
  real data already includes a trip to another time zone where a camera clock could
  easily be off by the local offset), and caption-based matches being
  trusted with no visual check at all, unlike every other path in the
  design.

All of the above is captured in `docs/USER_STORIES.md`, either as
resolved stories or explicitly-unresolved "Problem" notes — nothing here
was silently decided.
